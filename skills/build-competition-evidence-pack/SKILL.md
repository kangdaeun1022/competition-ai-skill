# Build the competition evidence pack

Take the selected problem/mechanism and claim IDs as the research boundary. Research supplies decision evidence; strategy changes go back to planning.

1. Register decisive claims about problem existence, demand, feasibility, differentiation and outcomes. Prioritize a claim that could overturn the proposal.
2. Search primary sources: official contest/agency publications, original papers, statistics with methodology, official patent records, and original dataset documentation. News is a discovery lead unless it directly establishes the reported event. Record queries, search dates and access failures.
3. For each source record source ID, title, URL/file, publisher, publication/update/check date, exact supporting location, population/timeframe, methodology, licensing/access and limits. Check newer relevant evidence and contradictions, not just supporting hits.
4. For each claim record claim ID, exact wording, source IDs, support status (supported/partial/contradicted/unknown), inferential limits and verification plan. Separate observations, assumptions, targets and causal assertions. Never convert a prototype result into impact evidence.
5. For data, distinguish catalog listing from actually acquired data; inspect schema/sample, missingness, collection dates, leakage, representativeness and allowed uses. For a paper, check baseline, sample and evaluation design. For patents, record jurisdiction, identifier, claims relevance and public legal status at check date; this is a preliminary screen, not a legal conclusion.
6. Reconcile conflicts explicitly. Downgrade or withdraw unsupported wording and identify affected claims/features for revalidation.

Deliver EVIDENCE_PACK.md with research scope, source register, claim matrix, contradiction log, data readiness and next smallest validation. Produce DOCX/charts only when requested and using available document tooling; label absent tools rather than claiming a generated file. Retain private source material locally unless external upload is authorized. Completion means every decisive claim is classified, not that every claim is supported.
