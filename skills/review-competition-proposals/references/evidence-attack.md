# Attack evidence
Trace decisive statements to original passages or actual experiment outputs. Verify dates, population, baseline, sample, method, uncertainty and whether the claim exceeds the source. Test alternative explanations, cherry-picking and conflicting evidence.
Compare tables, deck, narration and demo labels for drift. Flag invented numbers, plans presented as results and benchmark-to-impact leaps. An unreachable source is unverified, not automatically false. Return exact claim IDs, supported scope and the smallest repair; route new research to the evidence pack.
