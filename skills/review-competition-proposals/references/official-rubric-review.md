# Official rubric review
Compare the exact artifact version to current official sources. Account for every criterion and eligibility/format condition with source location, artifact evidence and supported/partial/missing/noncompliant status.
Check the chain problem→mechanism→user action→outcome. Distinguish missing proof from disproved claims. When scoring use the official weights and a declared scale, marking uncertainty; do not compensate a blocker with a high total. Findings include issue ID, affected criterion, severity, evidence, minimal remedy and completion condition.
