# Communication reality audit

## Purpose

Detect a submission that is technically defensible but cognitively expensive, generic, or obviously machine-polished. This is a communication audit, not a license to weaken evidence.

## Time-poor judge test

Review the artifact once using only the information a rushed evaluator is likely to notice first: title, opening section/slide, dominant visual, core number, demo moment and final ask/takeaway. Then answer without rereading:

- Who has the problem, at what exact moment?
- What fails today?
- What does this proposal actually do?
- What is demonstrably different from the nearest alternative?
- What one piece of proof should be remembered?

If these cannot be answered accurately, record a clarity defect even if the full artifact eventually explains them.

## Memorability diagnostic

Rate 0–5 as an internal diagnostic, not an official score:

0 indistinguishable; 1 generic category only; 2 problem remembered; 3 mechanism can be restated; 4 one distinctive proof/scene is remembered; 5 problem + mechanism + proof are recalled without exaggeration.

Improve memorability by specificity, contrast and observable proof—not slogans, fabricated drama or decorative novelty.

## AI-slop audit

Flag patterns that reduce credibility or specificity:

- abstract nouns replacing actors and actions;
- repeated `혁신적/효율적/통합적/맞춤형` claims without mechanism;
- long problem framing with no concrete decision moment;
- `AI 활용` without input → model/logic → output → human decision → failure path;
- every benefit stated only as an expectation;
- suspiciously uniform bullet rhythm and redundant three-part structures;
- generic market/team/ESG claims that answer no rubric question;
- polished causal or impact language stronger than the evidence register;
- invented precision, fake user quotes, fake interviews or fabricated metrics.

## Remedy discipline

Prefer the smallest edit that makes actor, moment, mechanism, evidence or limitation concrete. Preserve the author's natural voice where supplied. Do not deliberately introduce errors, slang or awkwardness merely to appear human.

## Output

Return `TIME_POOR_RECALL`, memorability diagnostic with reason, AI-slop findings by artifact location, and smallest edits. Findings join the normal review issue register and must not override official rubric priorities.
