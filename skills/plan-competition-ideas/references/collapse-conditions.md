# Collapse conditions
Register one to three assumptions whose failure invalidates the mechanism: user action, data permission, causal effect or operational owner. For each specify observable failure threshold and its basis, cheapest test, owner/deadline and fallback.
An untested assumption stays open. A failed condition reopens the affected selection gate and evidence claims; do not cover it with additional features. Freeze PROBLEM_LOCK only with explicit assumptions, excluded scope and a viable proof within team capacity.
